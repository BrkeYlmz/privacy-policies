Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Mosaic Blast ("the app") handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send any data over the internet.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your game progress: the best number of stars you earned on each level.
  • Your settings: whether sound and haptics are turned on.

This information never leaves your device and is not shared with the developer or anyone else. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, it may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, usage data or any other personal information. It contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics or tracking.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all of the information it has saved.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that use personal data, such as advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change.
