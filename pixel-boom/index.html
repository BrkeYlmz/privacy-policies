Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Pixel Boom ("the app") handles your information.

Summary

Pixel Boom is free to play and shows ads, which are provided by Google AdMob. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or other contact details. To show ads, Google AdMob collects some information from your device, as described below. Your game progress and settings stay on your device.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show ads. When the app requests or shows an ad, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks.
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it.
  • Information about your device and the app, such as the device model, operating system version and language.
  • How you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped.
  • Diagnostic information about how ads load and perform.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show ads that are more relevant to you. To learn more, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: The app asks whether it may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: The app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can review or change your choice at any time with the "Privacy Settings" button on the app's title screen.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following on your device:

  • Your game progress: the best number of stars you earned on each level.
  • Your settings: whether sound and haptics are turned on, and which gameplay hints you have already seen.
  • Your ad consent choices, where they apply.

This information is not sent to the developer. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, it may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Information the developer does not collect

The developer does not receive, store or sell any personal information about you. Apart from Google AdMob, the app contains no software development kits (SDKs) for analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all of the information the app has saved on it. Information collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, which explains how to access and delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using personal data in new ways, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change.
